**The released-data attribution defect and the improvement on corpus-rebuilt snippets survive this audit. The broader conclusions about argumentation need narrower wording and better controls.** We found a concrete error in the explanation of an alternative speaker mapping, inherited graph components that cannot test their advertised mechanism, and gaps in evaluation provenance. We found no critical arithmetic failure in the principal reported results.

## Findings after reviewer discussion

### 1. Medium: cross-speaker similarity cannot influence this graph

This is a proven property inherited from the upstream rules. Speaker XOR allows only one power variable to be true. OROUT bounds every reason by its side's power variable. Consequently an A reason and a B reason cannot both be selected. The similarity constraint `reason_A + reason_B + similarity <= 2` is therefore redundant; the nonnegative similarity score contributes a constant to both candidate decisions. The argument also holds under the corresponding LP constraints.

The preference-grounded classifier has another reduction: on all 450 Jev-scored Terra/Haiku/GLM examples, it agrees with selecting the side of the strongest reason whose rebuttal does not defeat it. These are valid implementations of their stated rules, but limited tests of richer argument interaction.

**Implication:** a similarity ablation cannot demonstrate whether useful similarity reasoning helps. The whole graph is not inert: within-side contradictions change the GLM result from 106 to 104 correct. Preserve the faithful reproduction, document these restrictions, and expose the equivalent simple baselines. Any redesigned graph is a new method requiring separate development.

Evidence: [factor graph](https://github.com/muttaqiahmadalladin/cs-577/blob/2e0af7fe7a1dd2be01503c9fa62e7ebdf9a7deaa/scripts/factor_graph.py#L33), [grounded relation](https://github.com/muttaqiahmadalladin/cs-577/blob/2e0af7fe7a1dd2be01503c9fa62e7ebdf9a7deaa/scripts/grounded_extension.py#L68), [independent verifier output](implementation/verification.json).

### 2. Medium: the alternative “majority speaker” reading is misdescribed

In **86 of the 128** last-line-consistent snippets, both displayed letters have the same real participant as their majority speaker. Thus assigning each letter its majority speaker generally does not map A/B to two distinct people. In snippet 0, displayed A contains 86 professor words and five other words; displayed B contains four professor words and three other words. Both majorities are the professor.

The reported **69/128** is nevertheless correct for a different statistic: whether the gold letter contains more of the professor's words. The conditional **7,596/13,185 = 57.6%** under the last-line mapping is also correct. Correct the explanation and report the ambiguous assignments. Do not discard those valid counts or conclude that the central corruption finding is false.

Evidence: [report's two readings](https://github.com/muttaqiahmadalladin/cs-577/blob/2e0af7fe7a1dd2be01503c9fa62e7ebdf9a7deaa/report/report.tex#L150), [actual statistic](https://github.com/muttaqiahmadalladin/cs-577/blob/2e0af7fe7a1dd2be01503c9fa62e7ebdf9a7deaa/scripts/analyze.py#L119), [coordinator spot-check](methodology-spot-check.json).

### 3. Medium: “does not improve” is stronger than the evidence

The bounded conclusion **“no demonstrated reliable improvement among the evaluated implementations”** is supported. Equivalence and general failure of argumentation are unproven. Some observed differences favor argument variants: Haiku/Jev reasons-only and preference-grounded each reach 73.3%, versus direct Haiku at 68.0%; Terra rebuttals add eight correct cases over reasons-only. Nonsignificant paired tests do not erase those point estimates or establish a small equivalence margin.

The report already acknowledges low sensitivity to small differences and uses appropriate no-evidence language later. Align its abstract and contribution with that limitation. Add paired uncertainty for graph/direct differences and predeclare a practically meaningful effect before a future confirmatory evaluation.

Evidence: [headline](https://github.com/muttaqiahmadalladin/cs-577/blob/2e0af7fe7a1dd2be01503c9fa62e7ebdf9a7deaa/report/report.tex#L45), [results and low-power qualification](https://github.com/muttaqiahmadalladin/cs-577/blob/2e0af7fe7a1dd2be01503c9fa62e7ebdf9a7deaa/report/report.tex#L309).

### 4. Medium: inference and generalization need the correct sampling unit

There are 150 snippets from 45 meetings, with professor counts **110, 23, 15, and 2**. Ordinary exact McNemar conditions on independent discordant examples; pairing methods within a snippet does not handle dependence across snippets from one meeting. Extend meeting-level uncertainty to the primary graph, judge, and ensemble contrasts. Meeting bootstrap still cannot establish generalization to new professors.

The policy search does select on development meetings and test on disjoint meetings. **No direct test-label leakage was established.** Its 500 overlapping splits are internal validation of a fixed policy family, rather than independent experiments or a pristine test of all earlier exploratory choices.

Longer context adds third-party speech in 148/150 longest windows, as well as more words and potential explicit role cues. The observed gain is credible; its mechanism is unresolved. Institutional professor labels operationalize role identification, with no independent behavioral-power labels. Use professor/non-professor language and harmonize task prompts before extending conclusions to conversational power.

Evidence: [sampling and limitations](https://github.com/muttaqiahmadalladin/cs-577/blob/2e0af7fe7a1dd2be01503c9fa62e7ebdf9a7deaa/report/report.tex#L352), [policy-selection code](https://github.com/muttaqiahmadalladin/cs-577/blob/2e0af7fe7a1dd2be01503c9fa62e7ebdf9a7deaa/scripts/grounded_policy_search.py#L73).

### 5. Medium: judge decisions are less stable than the prose suggests

The two case orders produce different predictions on **33/150 snippets (22%)**, despite only three additional correct answers in one order. Report both decision instability and aggregate accuracy. One call per order cannot distinguish a causal order effect from generation variability; same-order repeated calls are required. This static self-judging protocol also does not test every iterative debate procedure.

Evidence: [report wording](https://github.com/muttaqiahmadalladin/cs-577/blob/2e0af7fe7a1dd2be01503c9fa62e7ebdf9a7deaa/report/report.tex#L339), [saved judge answers](https://github.com/muttaqiahmadalladin/cs-577/blob/2e0af7fe7a1dd2be01503c9fa62e7ebdf9a7deaa/results/glm_run/judge.jsonl), [verification and affected IDs](statistics/verification.json).

### 6. Medium: collection and evaluation provenance need reconciliation

Terra's saved manifest describes rebuttal scoring as validity × grounding × countering; the primary analysis uses countering × grounding. The report states the primary formula, so the manifest mismatch does not establish that the headline formula is erroneous. Applying the manifest formula changes Terra IDs **74 and 110** while preserving 108 correct: matching accuracy can hide different predictions. All 150 exported GLM score rows omit the resolved scorer-model field.

The Terra collection script also depends on an undocumented historical template path, and the archived executed-script hash differs from the committed script. Add distinct collection/evaluation manifests and preserve executed artifacts, model versions, formulas, quantization, solver settings and per-example outputs. Keep alternative formulas exploratory.

Evidence: [manifest](https://github.com/muttaqiahmadalladin/cs-577/blob/2e0af7fe7a1dd2be01503c9fa62e7ebdf9a7deaa/results/wu_graph_terra/manifest.json), [current formula](https://github.com/muttaqiahmadalladin/cs-577/blob/2e0af7fe7a1dd2be01503c9fa62e7ebdf9a7deaa/scripts/analyze.py#L178), [collection path](https://github.com/muttaqiahmadalladin/cs-577/blob/2e0af7fe7a1dd2be01503c9fa62e7ebdf9a7deaa/scripts/wu_codex_pipeline.py#L327).

### 7. Low: one tied ablation and the attribution contrast need explicit handling

Terra reasons-only ID **142** has equal quantized A/B objectives. CBC chooses B in the current run; an A tie policy produces the historical 101 rather than 100 correct. A comparison of the selected binary power variables does not specify a tie policy between alternative optimal solutions. Set a deterministic policy and archive the objectives. No speaker-level ties were found in the three principal Jev-scored full graphs; their 108/105/104 counts survive.

The all-150 released/rebuilt contrast also recodes eight gold letters and reassigns letters in 22 examples. Describe it as combined reconstruction. Lead the cleaner attribution claim with the **128 cases preserving letters and gold**. Their improvement survives strongly; gold recoding does not explain it away. Request co-batching differs between conditions, whose possible effect is unmeasured.

## Positive findings that survive

Independent sensitivity analysis used 100,000 whole-meeting bootstrap resamples, seed 2026. Intervals describe paired differences in snippet-weighted accuracy conditional on these sampled meetings/professors.

| Contrast | Paired difference | Meeting-bootstrap 95% interval |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Rebuilt versus released, 128 invariant-letter/gold cases | +21.1 percentage points | +9.6 to +32.7 |
| Rebuilt direct Jev versus always-A, 150 cases | +14.7 points | +6.5 to +22.1 |
| Jev ±15 context versus original span | +13.3 points | +6.0 to +20.6 |
| Jev ±60 context versus original span | +18.0 points | +9.9 to +25.7 |

These checks strengthen the paper's data correction and descriptive context findings. They do not isolate every context mechanism or establish performance on unseen speakers. The source defect does not establish that the original paper's human participants saw the released malformed text; the target report correctly leaves that unknown.

## Implications for our existing quote-filter project

Preserve the locked 151-snippet negative table as a historical result on the released representation. Its interpretation now needs the upstream attribution caveat. The old generated arguments, quote annotations and scores refer to that representation; do not attach them to reconstructed transcripts and call that a clean-data experiment.

Our local populated 40-row audit is identified as an assistant proxy in `docs/human-tasks.md`; it is not an independent human validation of corrected-data evidence. A retrospectively frozen subset of these already-inspected 151 examples also cannot undo prior test exposure. The next confirmatory claim should use genuinely untouched meetings/speakers.
